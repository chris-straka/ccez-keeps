Keeps is a fast, offline-first notes app in the spirit of Google Keep.

Everything renders from the on-device database first, so your notes are
visible instantly — even in airplane mode. When you're back online, changes
sync quietly in the background and converge with your other devices, newest
edit wins, no duplicates.

• Offline create, edit, delete, and search — syncs on reconnect
• Checklists, colors, pins, archive, and trash
• Freehand drawings attached right inside a note
• Light markdown: bold, italic, strike-through, code
• Per-device logins you can rename, rotate, or revoke anytime
• Dark theme, haptic feedback, no ads, no trackers

Keeps syncs through a private backend that only you can reach. One sign-in
enrolls the phone; the browser never opens again.
